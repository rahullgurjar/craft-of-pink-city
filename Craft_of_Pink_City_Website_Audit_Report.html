Section 01

The Customer Perspective (UX & Conversion)

1. Hero Section Above-the-Fold CTA Mismatch High Friction
Problem: Hero buttons are "Explore Our Craft" and "Follow on Instagram". Commercial shoppers looking to buy have to scroll past 2 long sections to see bags.
Business Impact: Higher bounce rate from high-intent buyers searching "Buy Jaipur quilted bags".
Exact Solution: Set primary CTA to "Shop Handcrafted Bags" (smooth scroll to #products) and secondary to "Wholesale Quote".
2. Live Product Search Bar (Catalog Discoverability) Product Discovery
Problem: With 40+ handcrafted items, users currently only have category pills and must endlessly scroll to find a specific pattern.
Business Impact: Shoppers fail to find desired styles (e.g. "marigold", "safari cats", "laptop sleeve", "duffle").
Exact Solution: Add a real-time instant search input above the product grid with live match filtering.
3. Missing Social Proof & Customer Reviews Trust & Credibility
Problem: Zero customer ratings, verified buyer reviews, or Mehendi wedding favor testimonials.
Business Impact: First-time shoppers hesitate to send money or place bulk orders on WhatsApp without proof.
Exact Solution: Add a dedicated "Customer Love & Reviews" section with a 4.9/5★ badge and verified buyer quotes.
4. Multi-Item Order Friction (No WhatsApp Cart Drawer) Average Order Value
Problem: Ordering 1 Duffle + 2 Vanity Pouches requires sending multiple separate WhatsApp messages.
Business Impact: Caps order value and creates checkout fatigue.
Exact Solution: Implement a lightweight "WhatsApp Bag" wishlist drawer allowing multi-product checkout in 1 tap.
Section 02

The Expert Developer Perspective (SEO & Technical)

1. Schema.org JSON-LD Structured Data Critical SEO
Problem: No JSON-LD structured data in index.html (no Organization, FAQPage, or ItemList/Product schema).
Google Impact: Google cannot generate rich snippet star ratings, merchant carousels, or interactive FAQ dropdowns.
Exact Solution: Inject comprehensive Schema.org JSON-LD scripts for Organization, 6 FAQ accordions, and all 40+ products.
2. Missing sitemap.xml & robots.txt Google Indexation
Problem: The public/ folder lacks robots.txt and sitemap.xml.
Google Impact: Googlebot crawls inefficiently and may delay indexing new collection updates.
Exact Solution: Generate public/robots.txt and public/sitemap.xml pointing to canonical URLs.
3. Render-Blocking Google Fonts @import Core Web Vitals
Problem: src/index.css loads Google Fonts via @import at line 1, creating a waterfall request chain.
Google Impact: Delays First Contentful Paint (FCP) and Largest Contentful Paint (LCP) on mobile networks.
Exact Solution: Move fonts to index.html with <link rel="preconnect"> and display=swap.
